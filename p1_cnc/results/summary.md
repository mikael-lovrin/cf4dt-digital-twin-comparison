## 10. Known limitations
- Data appear SYNTHETIC (suspected): uniform features, no tool/time/unit ID, 20 negative VB_mm values; Wear_Class is a deterministic binning of VB_mm. Every conclusion concerns the method on this file, not a real machine tool.
- Pseudo-time is the rank of VB_mm, so VB is monotone in pseudo-time by construction. The time index is target-derived and is the SAME input for physics, data-driven and hybrid models; none of the three is an independent predictive test. The physics row is a trend fit.
- Features explain almost nothing: max|Pearson r|=0.059, RF CV R2 random=-0.022, blocked=-0.120 (features only, no time input).
- Held-out evaluation uses 400 rows in 4 contiguous segments. The hybrid-vs-linear CI is a leave-one-segment-out jackknife with only k segments as units (t with k-1 df): it is wide and should be read as indicative only.
- RUL is in pseudo-time units (PTU, 1 PTU = one sorted sample index), not hours or cycles. The record has 2000 PTU, so the emulated horizon is the record length; the forcing is not wrapped inside the horizon.
- Prior-predictive support: designs with simulated VB at 2000 PTU above 1 mm were rejected before fitting (28.0 % of training candidates). The prior is therefore truncated, not the design box; the GP, the calibration prior and the Sobol analysis all use the same truncation (GP-mean VB at the horizon). The truncation is data-independent, but the results hold only for this truncated prior.
- RUL truth (first sorted index with VB >= 0.30 minus stage time) is CIRCULAR: it comes from the same target the models were fitted to. The 'miss' values compare the conditioned prediction with that circular crossing; they are not validation.
- c_w (force per mm of wear) is NOT IDENTIFIED and was dropped from the sampler. The force-observation term is therefore absent from the calibration; forces do not enter the posterior. No posterior ratio is reported for it.
- MCMC: 32 walkers, 1000 burn-in, 5000 steps per stage with a DE-move/DE-snooker mixture. Minimum steps/tau = 15.0 (target >= 50): NOT met; chains are unconverged and posterior widths are provisional.
- RUL is censored at the 2000-PTU horizon in 49.4 % of Sobol samples and 0.0 % of 50%-life posterior draws; censored values enter the cost curves as the horizon value, so T* depends on the censoring.
- Sobol indices use the truncated prior with 1538 of 4096 base rows kept (a base row is kept only if all its D+2 Saltelli rows lie in the support). Indices are noisy at that size.
- Surrogate trained on fixed-step RK4 (dt=1 PTU). Against the reference RK45 ODE the max |difference| on 30 designs is 0.0003 mm (0.001 x VB_crit). The headline speed-up is against the same RK4 batch as round 1; the RK45 figure is reported separately.
- GP nugget (standardised units, cap 1e-6) = 1.00e-10; at its bound: False. The emulator is near-interpolating because the ODE is deterministic, so the GP sd is tiny (median held-out 9.23e-04 mm) and the band is set mainly by sigma_obs and the residual scale.
- Band scale c = 0.383 is fitted on forecast residuals of TRAIN rows beyond the 75% stage (399 rows). Held-out rows are not used for the scale. The physics trend-fit centres of the prior box use all training rows, a small data dependence.
- Posterior RUL is conditioned on the observed VB at each stage time (offset correction of each GP mean trajectory). GP variance and sigma_obs enter the likelihood and the coverage band, not the RUL draws.
- Costs c_p=1, c_f=5 and derating law (gamma=0.5, d_min=0.7) are assumptions. Derating values are computed at the grid argmin and are meaningful only when an interior optimum exists.
- Nominal constants (sigma_n, Ea/R, C_th, T_inf, eta, T rise) are fixed, not fitted; k1, k2, alpha, hA are fitted in the trend fit and calibrated, and vc is fixed at 1 in calibration.
- F_res = sqrt(CF_1^2+CF_2^2+CF_3^2) is a combination of feature columns, not measured Fx,Fy,Fz.
- Vibration/AE are single snapshot values per row; no windowed RMS/std.
