## Verified problems (each: file:line or metric, what is wrong, concrete fix)
1. main.py:60 and main.py:86-88 (physics_model.fit_baseline): pseudo-time t = arange(N) over rows sorted by VB_mm, so the physics model receives the target's rank as its input. Physics held-out RMSE 0.0039 vs data-driven 0.1114 is therefore a comparison of a model given the target ordering against one given no time input. Fig01 shows VB vs index is an almost straight line, i.e. the "physics" fit is a linear trend. Fix: give the data-driven and hybrid models the same time index (or compare against a linear VB~t baseline) before claiming physics beats data-driven; otherwise label the physics row as a trend fit, not a predictive model.
2. main.py:153 and uq_calibration.py:90 (run_emcee defaults also 24/300/600): 24 walkers, 300 burn-in, 600 steps. Spec asks for 32 walkers and 1000+ steps after burn-in. Metrics: mean_autocorr_time=55.2, so steps/tau ~ 11 (summary's own rule of thumb is >~50), acceptance 0.10. Chains are not shown to be converged and this is not flagged as a failure. Fix: nwalkers=32, nburn>=600, nsteps>=2000; write per-stage tau, acceptance and steps/tau to results/stage_diagnostics.json; report it as a failed/unconverged check if steps/tau<50.
3. metrics.json calibration.coverage95_heldout=1.0 and fig09: the 95% predictive band is ~0.2 mm wide against held-out residuals of ~0.01 mm and target std 0.099 mm. Full coverage with a band that wide is over-conservative, not calibration success. Fix: report mean interval width, and coverage at 50/68/95% nominal levels; check which term (GP sd_h or sigma_obs draws, sigma_obs log-sd 1.15) dominates the width and fix that rather than reporting 1.000.
4. uq_calibration.py:45-50 (cal_bounds, c_w prior uniform in [-1000, 1000]) and metrics.json calibration.posterior_to_prior_std_ratio.c_w=0.057: the ratio is inflated by an absurd prior. Posterior sd 33.1 around mean 0.28 (summary sec. 7) means c_w is unidentified, yet the schema field reads as information gain. Fix: use a physically bounded prior (e.g. uniform [0, 1] mm-scale or N(0, 0.5)), report absolute posterior sd, and state c_w as unidentified in the metrics field, not as 0.057.
5. uq_calibration.py:27 (rul_from_grid sets censored RUL to t_grid[-1]=3000 PTU) feeding sobol_analysis: RUL is censored in 15.4% of Saltelli samples (summary sec. 6), so the RUL Sobol indices (log_k2 S1=0.35, log_hA ST=0.43) are horizon-dependent. Fix: extend H_GRID to >=6000 PTU, or use VB_end and time-to-threshold with censoring-aware handling as the primary Sobol target.
6. main.py:109 vs main.py:88 and main.py:129 (speedup_x=521): GP training targets come from simulate_batch (fixed-step RK4, dt=1 PTU), while the physics fit and the held-out physics use solve_ivp RK45. The emulator therefore emulates a different solver, and the 521x speedup is measured against the RK4 batch, not the RK45 ODE the summary calls "per-run ODE". Fix: report max|RK4 - RK45| on the LHS design, and time solve_ivp per run for the speedup.
7. results/summary.md:95 (claim "Per-stage tau and acceptance are in metrics-adjacent run log"): no run log exists in results/. main.py stores per-stage tau/acceptance in stage_out but never writes it to disk. Fix: write stage_out (without the _rul/_T/_cost arrays) to results/stage_diagnostics.json.
8. main.py:162 and decision.py:optimal_replacement (T* = 389.5 PTU at 50% life, c_f/c_p=5 assumed): the optimal T* is determined entirely by the assumed cost ratio and by the circular RUL truth (rul_true = first sorted index with VB>=0.30 minus stage time). The 75% stage derating is clamped at the 0.7 floor (raw 1-0.5*0.652=0.674), so the derating law is a step, not informative. Fix: sweep c_f in {2,5,10,20} and report T* sensitivity; state that the "rul_true_in_95=yes" entries are circular, not validation.
