## Verified problems (each: file:line or metric, what is wrong, concrete fix)
1. Stressed decision limit is circular (main.py:352-358, 355; decision.py:24-44). T_STRESS = median of the no-action window peak over the same 300 posterior samples that are then used to score the policies, so P(exceed | no action) = 0.500 by construction (metrics decision_stressed.p_window_no_action = 0.5). "Derating beats no action" (3.06 vs 16.67 per min) then follows from the cost constants, not from the plant or from model validity. The headline metrics "decision" block (optimal_value 0.8, cost 3.06) reports this scenario, while the physically defined scenario (100 degC) gives cost 0. Fix: pre-register a stress that does not use the posterior: keep limit 100 degC fixed, scale measured currents by 1.2 and add +10 K to coolant over the 30-min window, replicate on held-out session 12. Report P(exceed) and the cost grid. Use the break-even rule as the informativeness test: action pays only if C_fail*(P_no - P_act) > C_act + C_thr*(1-d)*(H-tau), i.e. dP > about 0.017 at d=0.8, tau=0.
2. Runtime and MCMC convergence both fail the brief. metrics runtime_sec = 852.5 s vs the 600 s budget (run.log lines 95, 120, 145: MCMC 181.6 + 222.1 + 266.1 s). All three stages stop at the 6000-step cap with min steps/tau 44-47 and converged_50tau = false (metrics mcmc, mcmc_by_stage; summary section 7). uq_calibration.py:85-91 breaks at the cap, so the change-log claim "until every parameter has >= 50 tau" is not true for any stage. r1 item 6 is not fixed.
3. Predictive pm band is width-driven and biased (main.py:247 sig_pm = RMS of the free-run pm residual, which includes the bias, used as i.i.d. per-point noise in uq_calibration.py:113). Pooled 95% predictive coverage 0.993 vs prior-predictive 1.0, at mean width 30.1 vs 37.4 degC. But 50% coverage is 0.24 and 80% is 0.67 (nominal 0.5/0.8), parameter-only 95% coverage is 0.28, and bias is +6.6 / +7.9 / +8.9 degC at 25/50/75% (metrics interval_calibration.bias_pm_degC = 7.81). Fix: estimate model-error sd after removing the bias and with time correlation (AR(1) on the 60-s grid), report 50/80/95 coverage plus an interval score or CRPS, and remove the "calibration adds" sentence (see claims).
4. GP 2-sigma coverage 0.516 -> 0.980 is a reporting change, not a calibrated surrogate (surrogate_model.py:93-100 sets a per-output constant variance = max(MSE on design 2 - mean GP variance, 0); validated on a third 100-point LHS set of the same LPTN ODE). The fit is leakage-free, but: (a) it is emulator-vs-own-ODE error, not model-vs-data discrepancy, so the name "discrepancy" is misleading; (b) RMSE is unchanged (0.1198 degC), only the sd grows; (c) it has no effect on the posterior: the likelihood variance is sig_obs^2 + sd_GP^2 (uq_calibration.py:65), with sig_obs 14-17 degC against an sd_GP of order the 0.12 degC RMSE, and the pm band runs the ODE directly, not the GP. Fix: rename it emulator-variance inflation, report its scale factor and 50/80/95 coverage, and drop the claim that it addresses model discrepancy.
5. Observation noise makes the calibration nearly uninformative. Effective sig_obs = 17.5 / 15.3 / 13.9 degC (main.py:245-246, raw 4.3 / 3.0 / 2.8 x sqrt(IF)). At 75% the posterior/prior std ratios are 0.56-1.00 for five of six parameters; only k_cu (0.35) is informed (metrics posterior_to_prior_std_ratio). Scaling the variance by IF is a defensible tempering (equivalent to dividing the log-likelihood by IF), but the resulting posterior says little, and the summary does not say so. Fix: use a GLS likelihood with an AR(1) covariance on the 60-s residuals, or state plainly that four of six parameters are prior-like and that predictions rest on the priors.
6. Perfect foresight and session reuse are not disclosed. uq_calibration.py:108 and decision.py:26-29 simulate with the measured future torque, speed and coolant of the held-out session, so the decision assumes the future load is known. Session 68 is used for MCMC (main.py:292-333), for coverage (uq_calibration.py:115-118) and for the decision, although the report calls it held-out. Fix: add both points to the limitations, and check forecast coverage on a session not used for calibration.
7. Hybrid is still the worst model, and its residual RF is trained in-sample (main.py:262-264). NRMSE: physics 0.594, data-driven 0.357, hybrid 0.682; the regularised hybrid (0.621) is still worst of the three. The nominal LPTN is fitted on the same 8 sessions that supply the residual target. Fix: train the residual RF on out-of-fold residuals (session-grouped), and report whether the hybrid beats the data-driven model.
8. Identifiability is only partly reported. The 75% correlation matrix has |rho| > 0.7 for k_cu with G_YC (0.81), G_WT (0.70) and C_W (0.65) (metrics posterior_correlation), but the summary only states the rule and never names the ridge. Fix: name the ridge in section 7 and state that k_cu is the only parameter with a clearly reduced posterior.

- Section 7 bias: the prose quotes only +6.61 degC (25% stage); the 50% and 75% stages are +7.93 and +8.88 degC.
- Changes vs round 1, MCMC bullet: "until every parameter has >= 50 tau". The 6000-step cap was reached at every stage with converged = False.
- Section 8 "derating beats no action: yes" (stressed) is presented as a finding; it follows from P(exceed | no action) = 0.5 by construction (see problem 1).
- Section 5 status "FAIL (outside 0.03 of nominal)": 0.9803 is 0.0303 above 0.95. The discrepancy is over-coverage, not a spread failure; say so.
- Limitations: no mention that future inputs are assumed known or that session 68 is both calibration and forecast session (problem 6).
