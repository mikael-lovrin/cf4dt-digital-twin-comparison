## Verified problems (each: file:line or metric, what is wrong, concrete fix)
1. **Headline risk and decision extrapolate beyond the observed sweep and beyond the calibrated predictive.** main.py:178 builds `rpm_grid` over 300-3300 RPM, but calibration only used 25/50/75% of the sweep (max 2400 RPM at 75%). The 3300 RPM point was never observed. Summary §8 headline "P(exceed) at 3300 RPM = 1.000" and metrics.json `risk.value` (0.99977) come from that extrapolation. fig5 (75% panel): the 3000 RPM held-out point lies below the 95% band, and the 25% band misses 2100/2400/2700 RPM. The summary itself reports coverage 0.60-0.67. Fix: restrict the headline risk/decision grid to RPM <= max calibrated RPM (2400), or label 2700-3300 as extrapolation, and do not put the 3300 RPM risk in metrics.json.
2. **Decision is set by assumed costs and an assumed rebalance efficacy, and the optimum is not robust.** decision.py:15-22 (V_full=10, C_fail=500, h_fail=0.1, C_rb=200, H_amort=200, resid_frac=0.1, all assumptions). The 10% residual after rebalance (decision.py:21) is not measured anywhere; it makes "rebalance" win at every stage by construction. Only C_fail is varied (main.py:192). fig6 right panel: J is about 4.9-5.0 for 2100-2250 RPM, so the point optimum 2250 RPM is within noise of 2100 RPM. Fix: sweep V_full, h_fail and resid_frac in {0.1, 0.5, 1.0} (resid 1.0 = rebalance does not help) and report the optimum as a range; state the decision is conditional on these assumptions.
3. **Surrogate variance is a single global constant that drives the likelihood.** main.py:126 uses `sd_median_log` (median GP predictive std over the prior-wide LHS design, sigma_GP=0.2617 log) for every RPM and every posterior sample (uq_calibration.py:53, 61). Summary §7 says this is 132% of sigma_obs, so it dominates the posterior widths and the risk. The value is not local to the posterior region and is not validated there. Fix: evaluate the GP std once per stage at the posterior-median theta over the 11 RPMs (one `predict(return_std=True)` call), use it as a per-point sd_y, rerun, and report sensitivity to sd_gp (e.g. 0.05 and 0.26).
4. **MCMC chains are too short for the reported statistics.** main.py:38 uses 6000 steps with burn 1500, so 4500 post-burn steps. metrics.json `calibration.mean_autocorr_time` is 151.9 (75% stage), and the 50% stage has max tau 201.9. 50*tau = 10,095 > 4,500 (summary §7 says so). The posterior/prior ratios, identifiable combinations and coverage are still reported as results. Fix: set CAL_NSTEPS to about 12000 with burn 1500 (50 tau for max tau 202), check runtime stays under 10 min, or mark these numbers as not converged in metrics.json.
5. **Target-axis choice uses the test data.** data_loader.py:68-85 selects the axis by correlation over all Mis=0 unbalance rows, including all LOGO held-out groups and the calibration experiment M0.0_U20.4_A1B0. The margin (Z excess r=0.689 vs Y 0.263) is large, so the leak is probably harmless, but it violates the brief's no-leakage rule as written. Fix: either recompute the axis choice inside each fold, or state explicitly in summary §2 that the choice uses held-out rows and report that the same axis wins on the training rows alone.
6. **Sobol ranking is a prior-width artifact and partly an identity.** physics_model.py:23-28 sets log-uniform prior widths of about 9.2 (e), 11.5 (k, c) and 3.7 (m) in log units. The response is exactly linear in log e (summary §5 admits this), so S_e = ST_e by construction. Summary §6 presents "e dominates" as a finding with no caveat. Fix: state in §6 that Sobol indices are conditional on the prior box and that the e result follows from the model structure. Optionally rerun with a common width per parameter as a check.
7. **Narrative numbers in summary.md are hard-coded, not computed, despite "All numbers below are computed by main.py".** (a) summary.md:17 says "Z mean 0.021 g at 300 RPM to 0.070 g at 3300 RPM", but the data give Z Root Mean Squared means of 0.0209 and 0.0701 (Z Mean is about 0.002 and 0.000). The label is wrong, the values are right. (b) main.py:380 "COBEM 2025 setup, 1 kHz, 0.25 HP, two disks" is user context, not in the data. (c) main.py:438 "GP length-scale for log e hit its upper bound" is not written to any output (only the kernel string is logged, main.py:125). (d) main.py:457 labels sigma_obs as "(g, log)", but the value is a log-space sigma. Fix: compute or log (a)-(c), or move them to a clearly marked "from user / not computed" note, and correct the label in (a) and (d).
8. **CV comparison has no uncertainty.** main.py:75-103 is one LOGO split with one RF seed (SEED=0). Hybrid 0.0556 vs Pure Data (cond.+feat.) 0.0675 is not tested. Per group the hybrid loses in several cases (e.g. M0.5_U27.2: 0.054 vs 0.040; M0.5_U20.4: 0.053 vs 0.051; M1.5_U20.4: 0.048 vs 0.042). Also, the physics arm gets an in-data healthy floor (acknowledged in summary §4). Fix: repeat the RF over 5 seeds and report mean and sd of pooled RMSE, plus a paired per-group comparison (sign or bootstrap over the 18 groups).
