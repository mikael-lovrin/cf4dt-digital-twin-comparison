## Verified problems (each: file:line or metric, what is wrong, concrete fix)
1. pm is never in the calibration likelihood, and the magnet is not re-initialised at the decision instant. This is the most likely root cause of the +9..+11 degC bias. main.py:384 uses `calib["meas"][steps, :3]`; make_logpost has three channels. decision.py:59 sets `x_tc[:, :3] = meas[t_c, :3]` and leaves the magnet at the model value, although the measured pm exists at t_c. Evidence: bias +9.06 / +9.52 / +11.46 degC at 25 / 50 / 75 % (metrics interval_calibration; run.log), so it is already present at 25 %. MAP sits on the prior box in the hot direction at 50 % and 75 % (metrics map: G_TM = -0.5, C_M = +0.5). fig7 (75 % panel) shows the posterior median 7-8 degC above the measured pm inside the calibration window itself, around 2 h. The coverage pattern follows from the offset: with a 95 % half-width of about 12 degC (sd about 6), a 10 degC offset gives about 0.6 at 95 % and about 0.15 at 50 % under a Gaussian approximation; observed 0.66 and 0.06. The failure is location, not width. The same posterior gives bias -0.60 degC on session 12, so the offset is session-specific rather than a fixed magnet-model error. Fix: add pm to make_logpost for steps before t_c only, with its own AR(1) sd and rho; report a second variant that sets x_tc[:, 3] from the measured pm; report pm coverage and bias on the forecast window; repeat with leave-one-session-out over the 11 sessions.
2. The stress "action pays" result rests on a forecast that is biased +10 degC on its own session. The stress constants themselves are independent of the results: x1.2, +10 K and limit 100 match the r2 review's spec (decision.py:33-35, 43-51). But measured pm on session 68 peaks near 85 degC (read from fig7; the sampled rows of sessions 2, 7 and 12 only reach 36-48 degC, so session 68 was not checked directly), while the no-action posterior median peaks at about 94-98 degC (fig7, 25 % and 50 % panels). P(exceed | stress) = 0.343 (metrics decision_stress_prereg) is therefore probably inflated by the same offset. The informative flag (main.py:430-433) does not test for this. Session 12 gives P = 0 with max pm 48 degC, so the informative flag rests on one session. Fix: re-run the stress on the pm-calibrated posterior and on the variant from problem 1, report both, keep the rule and constants unchanged, and state the result whichever way it falls.
3. The AR(1) error model uses in-sample training residuals. main.py:284-288 builds sd_bf and rho from phys_train, the nominal fit on the same 8 sessions. In-sample vs out-of-fold free-run RMSE: winding 4.27 vs 6.01, tooth 2.97 vs 3.82, yoke 2.79 vs 2.47, magnet 7.07 vs 8.78 (run.log; metrics oof_physics_rmse_WTYM). The out-of-fold residuals are already computed (phys_oof) and unused, so sig2 and the pm band are optimistic for a new session. The AR(1) coefficient itself is estimated on training data only, which is correct. Fix: estimate sd_bf and rho from phys_oof residuals (bias-free, stride 12), report both, and use the out-of-fold version as the headline.
4. The three-session held-out ranking is driven by one session, and the hybrid claims do not follow from the data. Per-session RMSE (summary section 4, order sessions 2 / 12 / 68): physics 16.8 / 5.3 / 5.9, data-driven 5.1 / 5.7 / 7.4, hybrid 21.8 / 2.3 / 1.7. The hybrid is the best model on sessions 12 and 68 and the worst on session 2, which sets the pooled NRMSE (0.693). Fig3 shows that session 2 is an unusual regime: the sampled rows reach only 101 rpm with i_q p99 of 242 A, against 500-2000 rpm and 133 A in sessions 7 and 12. Measured pm stays at 37-46 degC while physics climbs to 78 and the hybrid to 88. The residual RF is trained on targets from 4-session fold models (larger residuals) and applied to the 8-session nominal model at test time, a distribution mismatch. Fix: report per-session bias and RMSE, use leave-one-session-out over all 11 sessions, and check whether the training sessions cover the session-2 regime.
5. The ridge sentence is wrong. main.py:895-897 (summary.md line 149) says the partners on the ridge are "the G and C terms named above". No G term is named, and the only pair above |rho| 0.7 is C_W-k_cu (+0.90). The next pairs are below threshold: C_W-G_WT 0.66, k_cu-G_YC 0.64, k_cu-G_WT 0.63, C_W-G_YC 0.60 (metrics posterior_correlation, 75 %). The r2 review's ridge numbers (k_cu-G_YC 0.81, k_cu-G_WT 0.70, C_W 0.65) do not reproduce from the r3 75 % matrix; the r2 metrics.json was overwritten, so the r2 values cannot be checked. Fix: build the sentence from ridge_pairs and list the top four pairs.
6. Posterior mass at the prior-box edges is not flagged. At 50 % and 75 % the MAP has G_TM = -0.5 and C_M = +0.5, both on the bound (metrics map; main.py:109 uses +-0.5). The converged flag does not catch this, and the "0 of 6 prior-like" statement (summary line 128) uses the 75 % stage only; at 25 % C_M (0.81) and G_TM (0.82) are prior-like. Fix: report, per stage, the fraction of samples within 0.02 of each bound; either widen the box and re-run, or state that the box binds.
7. The held-out sessions never reach the decision limit, so the risk layer has no observed outcome to test against. Held-out pm maxima: session 2 about 46 degC, session 12 47.8 degC (sample), session 68 about 85 degC (fig7). The no-action forecasts (P = 0.27 before session end at 25 %) therefore cannot be checked against an event. Fix: state in the summary that the risk layer is untested against outcomes. If a validation is wanted, fix a limit that the data cross before running, and report it as a second scenario.
8. Labelling and data-dependence. summary.md line 109 calls 3.507 / 2.744 / 2.569 "sigma (bias-free residual sd)"; these are sqrt(sig2), which include the emulator term (bias-free values are 3.498 / 2.741 / 2.567; main.py:849 uses C["sig2"]**0.5). The AR(1) logic at 60 s rests on an assumed 2 Hz sampling rate with no timestamp (data_loader.py:6, 12); if the true rate differs, rho and the observation spacing both change. Fix: relabel, and report rho at 30 s and 120 s as a sensitivity.

## Strengths (max 4)
- The stress is pre-registered in substance. The constants (x1.2, +10 K, limit 100) match the r2 review's spec, the window is limited to the decision horizon, and no threshold is derived from the posterior. The cost arithmetic checks by hand: no action = 1000 x 0.343 / 30 = 11.44; d = 0.75 from +15 min = (5 + 75 + 1000 x 2/300) / 30 = 2.89; break-even = (5 + 20 x 0.2 x 30) / 1000 = 0.125.
- The AR(1) GLS likelihood is exact. uq_calibration.py:85-100 uses the Markov factorisation, Q = r_1^2/s2 + sum (r_t - rho r_{t-1})^2 / (s2 (1 - rho^2)), and logdet = n log s2 + (n-1) log(1 - rho^2). rho and the bias-free sd come from training residuals (main.py:284-288). The 60-s stride is used consistently for rho, sd and the observation grid (main.py:382), and the calibration uses only data before t_c.
- Failures are reported as failures: coverage FAIL, posterior-only and prior-predictive baselines, per-stage convergence, and runtime 462 s against the 600 s budget (r2 was 852 s).
- Reproducibility is good: fixed seeds, a relative DATA path, run.log consistent with metrics.json (stage numbers, costs, runtime), the full schema plus round, changes_r3, and r2_vs_r3.

## What this project can and cannot claim (for the final report)
- Can: a physics LPTN fitted to stator temperatures, a GP emulator (R2 0.9999, RMSE 0.12 degC, about 19,700x faster than the ODE), a Sobol ranking in which k_cu dominates peak pm (S1 0.75), and an end-to-end decision pipeline that runs in 462 s; on three held-out sessions the physics model does not beat an input-only RF (NRMSE 0.59 vs 0.36).
- Cannot: claim calibrated pm forecasts (50/80/95 coverage 0.06/0.28/0.66 on one session, bias +10 degC, not reduced by calibration), or claim a derating recommendation (the stress "action pays" is informative on the calibration session only, its forecast is biased about +10 degC there, and the cost constants and perfect-foresight future loads are assumed).
- Cannot: claim the hybrid is best or worst in general (it is best on two of three sessions and worst on the third, which drives the pooled NRMSE), or that any held-out session tests the 100 degC limit (observed maxima 46, 48 and about 85 degC).
