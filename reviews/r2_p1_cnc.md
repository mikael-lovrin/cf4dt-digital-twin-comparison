## Verified problems (each: file:line or metric, what is wrong, concrete fix)
1. REGRESSION, coverage/band (main.py:45 H_GRID to 6000 PTU; surrogate_model.py GPSurrogate.fit; metrics interval_calibration): the 95% band is 1.387 mm wide vs about 0.2 mm in round 1, and coverage is 1.000 at 0.5/0.8/0.95. Root cause: the GP has a WhiteKernel nugget (bounds 1e-10..1e-1 in globally standardised units). Standardisation uses ysd of the whole 121-point grid, which is about 16 mm at 6000 PTU because of the tail. The fitted nugget is 3.7e-4, i.e. sqrt(3.7e-4)*16 mm = about 0.3 mm, which matches the reported median held-out GP sd of 0.336 mm (about 123x sigma_obs). predict(return_std=True) includes this nugget, so every point gets the same floor and the band covers everything. Fix: revert the GP and calibration grid to 0-2000 PTU (my read-only refit on the same 400 designs gave window RMSE 0.13 mm, nugget at its lower bound, median GP sd 0.07 mm). Standardise per grid column or model log VB; cap the nugget upper bound at about 1e-6. Then recheck coverage.
2. REGRESSION, surrogate validity (main.py:179-189, design_bounds in surrogate_model.py, metrics surrogate.rmse=1.012): the RMSE of 1.01 mm is driven by thermal runaway in the prior box, not by the emulator's typical error. In a read-only check of 400 LHS designs, 28% exceed 1 mm wear at 2000 PTU and the maximum at 6000 PTU is about 181 mm. The record's maximum is 0.376 mm. On the 0-2000 PTU window the same GP has RMSE 0.15 mm and median |error| 0.012 mm. The error is heavy-tailed, so one RMSE hides this. Fix: keep only designs with VB(2000) <= about 1 mm (prior-predictive rejection; about 72% kept), or narrow the log-k boxes. Report surrogate RMSE and R2 on the 0-2000 window separately from the full grid.
3. Decision optimum is pinned to the grid edge and the 'interior' flag is gamed (main.py:283 and :343, decision.py:optimal_replacement, metrics decision_checks): T* = 1997.5 PTU, which is index 798 of 799 on T_DECISION, so 'not an endpoint' passes at a 2.5 PTU margin. T* is identical for c_f in {2,5,10,20}, so cost_sweep_range=[1997.5,1997.5] is a degenerate sensitivity result, not robustness. The stressed scenario is also trivial (T*=2000, nontrivial=false). Fix: call the optimum interior only if cost(T*) is below cost at the largest T by a tolerance and cost rises after T*; otherwise write 'no interior optimum in [5,2000]'. Replace the sweep with one that actually moves T* (e.g. c_f in {2,5,10,20} on a grid that extends past the record), and state that the decision depends on censored RUL.
4. MCMC still not converged, and the round-1 fix only changed the run size (main.py:48, uq_calibration.run_emcee): min steps/tau = 10.9 with 5000 steps. Mean tau rose from 55 (r1) to 370, and per-parameter tau reaches 460 (metrics mcmc.tau_per_param). Reaching 50 tau would need about 22,000 steps. The run.log warns on all three stages. The 'converged_50tau: false' flag is honest, but 'interval/mcmc fixed' is not true. Fix: remove the weakly identified parameters from the sampler (see 5), or use a DE-move mixture or reparametrisation; otherwise keep the flag and report the chains as unconverged in the headline.
5. c_w: data-derived prior, still unidentified, ratio still reported as information (main.py:231 cw_max from training F/VB ranges; metrics calibration.posterior_to_prior_std_ratio.c_w=0.469 with posterior sd 58 vs prior sd 124). The round-1 request was to report c_w as unidentified, not as a ratio. Fix: fix c_w to a constant or give it a physical prior that does not use the data (e.g. N(0, 50)), or drop it from the calibration. Report c_w as 'not identified' in the metric field, not as a ratio.
6. Posterior RUL is biased late and not conditioned on the observed wear at the stage (main.py:254-263, uq_calibration.posterior_grid, rul_from_grid): the 50%-life posterior median RUL is 2035 PTU (crossing near 3035), while the record's crossing index is 1715 and the LS trend crosses at about 1700. All three stages are late by 600-1300 PTU (25%: 1842 vs 1215 circular truth; 75%: 878 vs 215). Median RUL is computed from GP mean trajectories with no conditioning on VB at t_s. The 'truth in 95%=yes' entries are therefore trivially true because the 95% interval spans [0, 5000]. Fix: condition each posterior trajectory on the observed VB at t_s (offset or residual-corrected), then compare predicted crossing time with the empirical crossing for each stage and report the miss.
7. Physics and hybrid are not better than a trend (main.py:144-163, metrics baselines): the LS physics (held-out RMSE 0.00386 mm) is worse than the linear VB~t baseline (0.00308 mm). The hybrid gain of -0.00162 mm comes from an RF on t that smooths the residual over time, not from the ODE. The CI (i.i.d. row bootstrap over 4 held-out blocks) is called optimistic in the limitations but is still stated as 'excludes zero'. Fix: report the hybrid against the linear baseline, use leave-one-segment-out for the CI, and say the physics adds nothing over trend on this file.
8. Summary header is false and the headline speed-up changed reference (main.py:679 header 'No number below is typed by hand'; main.py:874 round-1 column typed; metrics surrogate.speedup_x=95418): section 9 round-1 values (c_w '+/-1000', round-1 speed-up, T*=389.5) are typed, and the header claims otherwise. The headline speed-up moved from 521x (RK4 batch) to 95418x (per-run solve_ivp at 6000 PTU, about 2 s per run) without a like-for-like rerun. The periodic forcing wrap at 2000 PTU (physics_model.py _rhs_factory, np.mod(t,N)) creates a kink that the GP and the RUL extrapolation must absorb; this is only a limitation sentence. Fix: generate the round-1 column from metrics_round1.json, change the header, report the speed-up against the same RK4 batch as well as RK45, and move the wrap issue into the verified problems of the surrogate.
